# Consent for the first-time lifter study

Read aloud before collecting observations:

> We’re trying six tasks in Ironvane to see where the app is easy or hard to use. We’ll use a disposable profile with pretend training data. You won’t need to lift anything or enter your name, email, account credentials or medical details. I’d like to note which tasks you finish, how long they take, what goes wrong and a short comment in your own words. I won’t record your screen, voice or face. Taking part is optional. You can skip any task or stop at any time. The project may publish anonymous totals and paraphrased observations. Is it okay to start?

Wait for an explicit yes. Record only the session code and yes on the private sheet. With a no, end the session and take no notes. If the participant withdraws during the session, discard that session’s notes. Clear the disposable profile between participants. Keep completed sheets private. Share only aggregate results and anonymous paraphrases in the repository.
