## Word order and spelling

Position and grip before equipment, then movement: 인클라인 덤벨 벤치프레스,
클로즈 그립 바벨 벤치프레스, 시티드 케이블 로우. Where two setups combine,
retain both qualifiers. Parenthetical attachments, gender, camera views and
version numbers remain distinguishable. Body parts in mobility names use
ordinary Korean (어깨, 가슴, 종아리, 허리). Exercise identity and canonical IDs
never change; instructions, custom names and persisted workout data are untouched.

Established gym loanwords use Hangul. Latin letters are restricted to EZ바,
T바, V바, V업/V시트, L시트/L풀업, letter-shaped T/Y/W raises, SZ바, JM
(the named press abbreviation), and Hyght (an uncommon proper-name spelling).
Do not label any translation native-reviewed before the named review.

Write the four standard press compounds solid: **벤치프레스, 숄더프레스,
레그프레스, 체스트프레스**. Keep other two-word names spaced, including
오버헤드 프레스, 프렌치 프레스, 카프 프레스 and 밀리터리 프레스.
Write angles as **45도**, never 45°. The 45-degree sled staples are
45도 레그프레스 and 45도 카프 프레스.

Keep distinguishing source words even when a label is unusual:
“incline breeding” is 인클라인 덤벨 브리딩, separate from 인클라인 덤벨 플라이;
“front chest squat” is 바벨 프론트 체스트 스쿼트, separate from 바벨 프론트 스쿼트.
Use 케이블 랫풀다운 (풀 레인지) for the full-range variant.

Two IDs may share a Korean title only when their English names normalize to
the same string after case folding, hyphen/punctuation removal and plural-s
normalization. Otherwise retain the differing source qualifier. Identical
upstream names and spelling-only variants remain upstream duplicates.
