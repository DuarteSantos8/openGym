## Style

Write titles in sentence case with no final full stop, the way the English
source is written: `жим штанги лёжа`, not `Жим Штанги Лёжа`. Keep the order the
English title uses — equipment first, then the movement, then the qualifiers —
so the list stays scannable next to the untranslated original the app shows in
parentheses.

Keep established loanwords that a Russian lifter says out loud: `бёрпи`,
`пилатес`, `кроссовер`, `гакк-приседание`, `EZ-гриф`, `машина Смита`. Do not
invent a Russian calque where the loanword is what the gym actually uses.

Use `ё` where it belongs (`подъём`, `лёжа`, `бёрпи`) — the rest of the Russian
UI pack does.
