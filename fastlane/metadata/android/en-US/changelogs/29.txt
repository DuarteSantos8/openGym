openGym is on F-Droid! Built from the same source as the APK and the Play app, without anything from Google: the gym card scanner uses the camera in the app, and Wear OS watches (which need Google Play services) are left out. Amazfit watches work through your own server.

New in 1.4.4: Amazfit (Zepp OS) watches, a fresh coat of paint all over, every set held against last time, and lots of fixes. Bugs and ideas go to GitHub or Discord.
